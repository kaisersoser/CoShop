CoShop privacy notice

Effective 10 July 2026.

Guest use

Shopping lists, prices, preferences, and photos are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. They are not sent to CoShop unless you explicitly sign in to use backup or sharing.

Account and sharing

When you sign in, Supabase processes your email address, session, household membership, lists, items, and any photos you choose to attach. Household members can see content belonging to that household. Private row-level and storage policies prevent access from other households.

Control

You can export list data from Backup & share. You can remove individual items and lists with undo. Signing out leaves local work available. To request cloud-account deletion while self-service deletion is being completed, contact the repository owner through the project’s GitHub issue tracker.

Retention and security

Deleted records are soft-deleted for recovery and operational consistency; the scheduled purge window is 30 days. CoShop does not sell shopping data or use it for advertising. Publishable client credentials grant no administrative access.
